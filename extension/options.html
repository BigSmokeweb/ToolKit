LEAD DESK CAPTURE

Save the useful part.

On LinkedIn People results, click the pinned LD icon once to add all displayed people to your paired Lead Desk. On an individual profile or post, select relevant text first for AI-assisted capture.

Use your local address or the exact HTTPS showcase URL without a trailing slash. A showcase needs permission for that address and a code from the owner.

What gets shared?

People capture sends the displayed result-card details and profile URLs to your paired app without using AI. Individual profile/post capture sends only your selected text and source URL, then uses your configured Gemini model. New leads remain unapproved and unverified.

This extension does not read LinkedIn passwords or cookies, crawl pages, or send invitations or messages. It has no automatic capture schedule.

The extension pairing token stays in this browser’s local extension storage. It is separate from your Gemini key and LinkedIn token.
