LEAD DESK CAPTURE

Save the useful part.

Select a person’s name, headline and relevant post in LinkedIn. One click on the pinned LD icon classifies and saves it in your paired Lead Desk.

Use your local address or the exact HTTPS showcase URL without a trailing slash. A showcase needs permission for that address and a code from the owner.

What gets shared?

Only the text you select and the profile/post URL are sent to your paired app. Lead Desk sends that selection to your configured Gemini model for extraction and classification. New leads remain unapproved and unverified.

This extension does not read LinkedIn passwords or cookies, crawl pages, or send invitations or messages. It has no automatic capture schedule.

The extension pairing token stays in this browser’s local extension storage. It is separate from your Gemini key and LinkedIn token.
